Alterome Therapeutics, Inc., a clinical stage biopharmaceutical company pioneering the development of next generation, small molecule targeted therapies for the treatment of cancer, today announced the dosing of the first patient in AKTive-001, a Phase 1/1b trial of ALTA2618 in adults with AKT1 E17K-mutant advanced solid tumors.
