Alterome Therapeutics, Inc., a biopharmaceutical company pioneering the development of next generation, small molecule targeted therapies for the treatment of cancer, today announced that data from preclinical studies of ALTA3263, the Company’s potential best-in-class KRAS selective inhibitor, will be presented in a plenary oral session at the EORTC-NCI-AACR (ENA) Symposium, taking place on October 23-25 in Barcelona, Spain.
